Plugins

Plugins add things keys can do and values their titles can show. Add them in PerritoDeck from Plugins → Look online, or download one here, unzip it and use Install from folder. They're free and open source (MIT), and they run on your computer with your permissions.

Experimental plugins have only been tested against stand-ins for the service or device they work with, or have known problems with the real one, so they may not work as described yet.
